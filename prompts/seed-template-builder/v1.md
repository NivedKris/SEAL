You turn a de-identified US medical transcription (MTSamples, CC0) into a structured outpatient encounter template for a fictional private hospital in Dubai, UAE. The template is synthetic training data for an insurance-claim checker.

Rules:
- Keep the original clinical content: the same condition, history, findings and reasoning. Adapt only the setting (Dubai outpatient clinic or emergency department), units (SI) and names (remove all names, dates, places and identifiers).
- Write as a careful clinician would, so that EVERY order is clearly justified by something in the note. Gaps are injected later by removing segments; you must write the complete version.
- "complaintSegments" is the complaint as told by the patient (history of presenting illness), 2–6 short segments of one or two sentences each, written in the third person ("Patient reports…").
- "findingSegments" are examination and result findings, 2–6 segments.
- Tag each segment with the documentation concepts it establishes, using ONLY these ids:
  - symptom_duration — states how long the symptom has lasted
  - conservative_treatment_failed — names a first treatment tried (rest, analgesics, physiotherapy…) and that it did not help
  - neurologic_deficit — objective neurological deficit on examination (weakness, reflex loss, sensory loss, positive straight-leg raise with radicular signs)
  - new_or_progressive_deficit — a deficit that is new or worsening since a previous assessment
  - red_flag_symptom — red flags (bowel/bladder dysfunction, saddle anaesthesia, fever with back pain, weight loss, trauma in elderly, history of cancer)
  - signs_or_symptoms_present — any symptom or sign that motivates investigation (use for most complaint segments)
  - qualifying_diagnosis_documented — explicitly documents a condition that qualifies a test (e.g. "known CKD stage 3", "osteoporosis on DEXA")
  - reason_for_repeat_test — explains why a test is repeated early (e.g. "therapy changed 6 weeks ago, HbA1c repeated to assess response")
  - medication_change — a medicine started or dose changed
  - neuro_manifestations — neuropathic symptoms such as numbness or tingling
  - pain_score_baseline — a numeric pain score
  - pain_score_improvement — a pain score improved versus baseline
  - significant_change_in_condition — unexpected change in condition or no response to therapy
  - previous_investigation_inconclusive — an earlier test was inconclusive
- A segment may have zero, one or several concepts. Do not tag a concept the text does not clearly state.
- Diagnoses: valid ICD-10-CM codes at full billable specificity (e.g. "M54.50", "E11.65"), with exactly one primary. Use the most specific code the note supports.
- Orders: choose ONLY from the allowed codes listed for this theme. Order what the note justifies (typically 1–6 orders). Give each order a short reason that points to a segment.
- "plan" is one or two sentences.
- Output one JSON object matching the schema. No markdown.
